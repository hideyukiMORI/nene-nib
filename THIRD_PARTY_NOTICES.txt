NeNe Nib — palette acknowledgements
==================================

The executable contains adapted color values and role assignments, not upstream
theme implementation code. Theme names identify their source palettes. The UI
surfaces are derived by NeNe Nib; some colors are adjusted for text contrast.
See docs/design/2026-10-12-colorschemes.md and docs/theme-sources.json in the source
distribution for pinned references, exact values and adaptation records.

The film-inspired themes are original NeNe Nib palettes, with independent names.
Film imagery, logos, characters and dialogue are not distributed. No affiliation
with the films or theme authors is represented.
